A little guide to candle care

Simple fire-safety and candle-care tips to help you enjoy every glow with care. Always follow the instructions provided with your candle.

Fire safety · A quick read

A safer, softer glow

Keep these simple precautions in mind whenever you light a candle at home.

Read the tips
  • Keep a burning candle within sight, and extinguish it before leaving the room or going to sleep.
  • Place it on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from drafts and anything that can catch fire.
  • Keep candles out of reach of children and pets, and leave space between burning candles.
  • Never move a burning candle or pour water on hot wax. Extinguish the flame carefully and let the vessel cool before handling.

Candle care · A quick read

Make the first burn count

A little patience at the beginning can help your candle burn more evenly over time.

Read the tips
  • Set your candle on a level, heat-resistant surface away from moving air.
  • For an even melt, let the wax melt across the surface on the first burn, while following the candle’s care label.
  • Keep each burn to no more than four hours, or less if the candle’s instructions say so.
  • Let the candle cool completely before lighting it again.

Candle care · A quick read

Keep your candle at its best

Small habits between burns help keep your candle tidy and ready for your next moment of ikoi.

Read the tips
  • When the candle is completely cool, trim the wick to about ¼ inch before lighting, unless its care label advises otherwise.
  • Keep the wax free of wick trimmings, matches and other debris.
  • Stop burning when about ½ inch of wax remains, or sooner if the candle’s instructions recommend it.
  • Do not use a candle if its vessel is cracked or damaged.
