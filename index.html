Why Ikoi exists

憩い ikoi (noun, Japanese)
Rest and relaxation; a pause to breathe and feel at ease.

Ikoi grew out of an experience on a personal healing journey. It is a small studio making handmade soy wax candles, made to bring calmness, peace, joy and relief to the people who light them.

Every candle can be customised to any scent you prefer, so it can suit a quiet night in, a romantic date, or a gift for someone else or for yourself.
