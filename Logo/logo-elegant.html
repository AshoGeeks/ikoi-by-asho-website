1. Line wordmark

Fine, widely spaced lettering. The first I is the candle, with a slim flame above it.

2. Crest

A double ring around a candle on a dish. Suits jar lids and stickers.

3. Arch

A candle in a fine-line arch that mirrors the arched photo on your site.

4. Ensō

An open circle, the Japanese symbol of calm, with a flame rising from the gap.

5. Seal

A solid stamp with the kanji 憩 (ikoi, rest) and a flame above it.
